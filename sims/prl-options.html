Explain: PRL Line – PRL Option’s
Tap a panel to start a flush and to show its charts below. Red glow = positive pressure, blue glow = suction, yellow dots = air relieved through the PRL, small U under each slab = trap seal. Drawn levels: option 1 shows 5 levels of the building (simulated floors are written beside each), options 2 and 3 show floors 3, 2 and 1.

    Limits: quasi-steady air-entrainment model with a dynamic surge multiplier and a damped trap oscillator. PRL option factors (1 best, 2 slightly less, 3 least) are illustrative for comparison, not test data. Not calibrated to AIRNET or site data. Use it to compare options (PRL size, vent, stack size), not for final design. Confirm with on-site pressure readings.
